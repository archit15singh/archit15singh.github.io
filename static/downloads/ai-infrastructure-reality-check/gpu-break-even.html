Model and assumptions

The cost of capital annualizes the installed cost over the economic life at zero resale value. Fixed operations and baseline power are charged over 8,760 calendar hours. Incremental electricity is charged for billable hours at the difference between loaded and idle IT power, multiplied by PUE and the energy tariff. Include recurring demand charges and capacity fees in fixed operations if applicable.

The model uses steady-state revenue and assumes billable hours draw loaded power. It leaves out taxes, contract ramp timing and resale proceeds. A realized rate accounts for discounts and credits. An annual economic surplus includes the capital charge and differs from annual cash flow.
