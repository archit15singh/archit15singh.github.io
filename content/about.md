I'm Archit Singh. I build tools for AI agents, with a particular interest in how they preserve context, recover from failures and make their actions inspectable.

[Memori](/projects/#memori--flagship) explores persistent memory for coding agents. [Luffy](/projects/#luffy) applies those ideas to PR review, keeping an execution trace and repository-specific memory after each run. I also contribute [security detection fixes](/research/#3--ai--security).

I write about the decisions behind these systems, including approaches that failed and limits I have not resolved. In September 2025, I presented [Memory is the Agent](/speaking/) at PyCon India.

You can [explore the projects](/projects/), [read the technical notes](/research/) or [contact me](/contact/) about a talk, collaboration or relevant engineering role.
