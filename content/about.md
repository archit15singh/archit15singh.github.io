I'm Archit Singh, a Senior Software Engineer at [Abnormal AI](https://abnormal.ai/). I build AI agents and detection systems for email security, working across detection engineering and the backend systems that support it.

My central interests are how to detect threats, investigate failures and make security decisions reliable in production. Abnormal's platform processes billions of email messages through a layered detection pipeline, with agents handling its hardest cases. That is company-wide scale, described in [Abnormal's public architecture article on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/abnormal-ai-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-for-agentic-email-security-at-scale/).

Outside work, I build independent tools that explore related engineering questions. [Lorevell (formerly Memori)](/projects/#lorevell) keeps knowledge in Markdown files that humans and agents can read, edit and reuse. [Luffy](/projects/#luffy) is a PR review agent with repository-specific memory and execution traces. I also build [FootagePilot](/projects/#footagepilot), an AI-assisted video-editing tool, and contribute [public security detection fixes](/research/#3--ai--security).

I write about the decisions behind these systems, including approaches that failed and limits I have not resolved. In September 2025, I presented [Memory is the Agent](/speaking/) at PyCon India.

You can [explore the projects](/projects/), [read the technical notes](/research/) or [contact me](/contact/) about a talk, collaboration or relevant engineering role.
