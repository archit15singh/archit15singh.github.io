# Meme template fit-note menu

The searchable space for template selection. Match the *rhetorical move* of your angle to the "use when" column — **read the whole menu and pick the closest fit; do not default to the same few (Drake / Two Buttons / Always Has Been).** Use three *distinct* templates per batch, and vary them across runs. `boxes` = exact number of caption strings the template needs.

Ids come from Imgflip's `get_memes`; regenerate this menu if the bank drifts. Names/ids are stable for the classics below.
